# Initial RUP Approval Workflow

## 1. Plan Creation

- Initial RUP (Range Use Plan) is created and saved.
- **Status:** `Staff Draft - SA`

---

## 2. Plan Submission by SA

- SA submits the plan.
- **Status changes to:** `Add Content to RUP - AH`
- The plan is assigned to AH to add the required RUP content.

---

## 3. AH Updates the Plan

- AH updates the plan.
- AH may save changes at any time without changing the workflow status.

### AH Submission Process

Before submitting, AH adds the required description and then chooses one of the following submission options.

---

# Option 1: Submit for Final Decision

- AH selects **Submit for Final Decision**.
- The initial RUP is submitted.
- **Status changes to:** `Awaiting Decision - SA`
- The plan is sent to SA.

## SA Decision Options

### 1. Recommend Ready

- SA selects **Recommend Ready**.
- **Status changes to:** `Approval Recommended - DM`
- The plan is sent to DM.

#### DM Options

1. **Approve**

   - **Status changes to:** `Approved`

2. **Not Approved – Further Work Required**
   - Further work is required before approval.
   - _(Workflow after this decision is to be determined.)_

---

### 2. Recommend Not Ready

- SA selects **Recommend Not Ready**.
- **Status changes to:** `Approval Not Recommended - DM`
- The plan is sent to DM.

#### DM Options

1. **Approve**

   - **Status changes to:** `Approved`

2. **Not Approved – Further Work Required**
   - **Status changes to:** `Changes Requested - AH`
   - The plan is returned to AH for corrections.
   - AH may update the plan and repeat the submission process by choosing either:
     - **Submit for Final Decision**, or
     - **Submit for Staff Feedback**.

---

### 3. Request Changes

- SA selects **Request Changes**.
- **Status changes to:** `Awaiting Input - AH`
- The plan is returned to AH for corrections.
- AH may update the plan and repeat the submission process by choosing either:
  - **Submit for Final Decision**, or
  - **Submit for Staff Feedback**.

---

# Option 2: Submit for Staff Feedback

- AH adds the required description.
- AH selects **Submit for Staff Feedback**.
- **Status changes to:** `Awaiting Feedback - SA`
- The plan is sent to SA.

## SA Feedback Options

### 1. Request Changes

- SA selects **Request Changes**.
- **Status changes to:** `Awaiting Input - AH`
- The plan is returned to AH for updates.
- AH may update the plan and repeat the submission process by choosing either:
  - **Submit for Final Decision**, or
  - **Submit for Staff Feedback**.

### 2. Recommend for Submission

- SA selects **Recommend for Submission**.
- **Status changes to:** `Recommended For Submission - AH`
- The plan is returned to AH.
- AH may update the plan and repeat the submission process by choosing either:
  - **Submit for Final Decision**, or
  - **Submit for Staff Feedback**.
